Veterans experience underemployment more often than their peers and face unique challenges when it comes to translating their experience into terms that corporate America can understand.

Our founder totally gets it. Jeff, a Marine Corps veteran, was able to make a successful career change thanks to a single manager's open-mindedness and willingness to see the value of his transferable skills. Through his own lived experience, Jeff knows what it takes to make relevant military experience understandable to non-vet hiring managers and recruiters.

We offer a coding boot camp and high-touch job placement services for vets, and training for non-veteran hiring teams. Our goal is to reframe the conversation around preconceived notions of an ideal candidate and make it easier for veterans to switch their code to better match that of hiring teams. We're a small team of passionate people who are committed to helping veterans find meaningful employment.

How we're organized

Hired Labs, Inc. is an independent 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Stiles Hall serves as our fiscal agent. They handle our banking, accounting and mailing address, and connect us with their community. That's why our donation form names Stiles Hall.

Leadership

Jeff Stock

Founder and CEO

Jeff, a US Marine Corps veteran, served for nearly a decade as an interpreter and intelligence collector in the Former Soviet Union, Europe, and the Middle East. He led a team of signals intelligence collectors in and around Fallujah, Iraq during Operation Iraqi Freedom (OIF) II & III, then served as an interpreter for the Defense Threat Reduction Agency (DTRA). After completing his service, Jeff earned a B.A. in Linguistics at UC Berkeley, and then went on to build a successful career in tech creating and leading data analysis and operations teams.

Passionate about helping veterans gain confidence in their abilities and express themselves effectively in environments where veterans are often misunderstood, Jeff founded Hired Labs where he leverages his experience in helping people from diverse backgrounds navigate the complexities of corporate America.
