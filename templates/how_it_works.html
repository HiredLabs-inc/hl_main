{# Boot camp #}

Coding boot camp

Go from zero to creating functional web applications using Python in six weeks.

We'll show you the basics, then guide you through building a full-stack web application. You'll learn Python, JavaScript, HTML, CSS, and frameworks like Django and React. Along the way we'll show you how to use Git, text-generating AI and other tools.

At the end, you'll have your own website with a functioning backend, and the knowledge to customize all of it and keep building.

No experience required

If you can read and write emails, you can code. Even if you've never written or read any code, this boot camp is for you.

No math required

Math is useful, but not required to write functional code. We focus on practical skills and solving real problems.

No laptop required

We build in the browser with free tools, so a library or internet cafe computer works. A phone is possible, but tough.

Commitment required

Attend both weekly classes for six weeks. To really learn, make coding a daily habit.

Is it right for you?

Yes, if you've never coded but have thought about a boot camp.

Probably, if you've just started learning and aren't sure what to learn next.

Maybe not, if you're already experienced. We'd still love to meet you and point you in the right direction.

{# Career support #}

Career support

High-touch, personalized help to land your next job: resume updates, a tailored job search and application tracking. The goal is to get called back for a first interview.

Optional 6-week extension. Stay actively engaged and you can continue for another six weeks, with bi-weekly check-ins on your progress.

Sign up for career support
  1. 1

    Intake call

    A quick call about your background, experience and what you want in your next role.

  2. 2

    Resume updates and job search

    Our specialists update your resume and send a tailored list of open roles that fit you.

  3. 3

    Apply

    You apply to the jobs using your updated resume.

  4. 4

    Record results

    Track applications and outcomes in a simple spreadsheet, including jobs you find on your own.

  5. 5

    Rinse and repeat

    Based on your feedback, we send new openings and keyword-optimized resumes for jobs you really like.

{# Eligibility #}

Who we serve

Our programs are built for enlisted veterans of the U.S. military.

  • Eligible: U.S. military veterans who completed active duty at a rank between E-1 and E-9, with service characterized as anything other than dishonorable or uncharacterized.
  • Waitlist: veterans who served as commissioned or warrant officers are welcome to apply, and are enrolled as space becomes available.
  • Not eligible: non-veterans and veterans of countries other than the United States.
